About the data

Source: basin water-use statistics for 2004–2023 (21 water-use types), converted from thousand m³ to mln m³. All analysis is done in the source Jupyter notebook; this page only displays its output.

Efficiency = water used ÷ water withdrawn; loss rate = transport losses ÷ withdrawn. ▲/▼ compare the selected year with 2004.

Statistics cover 5 of the 8 water management basins; southern basins are hatched.
